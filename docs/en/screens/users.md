# Users

[Русская версия](../../ru/screens/users.md) · [Work screens](../user-guide.md)

Open **Users** at `/app/report/ad.users.v2`. Select a domain and check the
snapshot time shown for the selected scope.

1. Use **Require review** to open a check and inspect the affected accounts.
   Search **All accounts** by account or name. Use **Privileged** to review
   membership, including the composition on a selected date when offered.
2. Open an account. Review **Account**, **Rights**, **Activity** and
   **Changes**. In Rights, inspect group paths. In Activity, check the time
   and source and use the account-specific Journal or Problems link if
   available.
3. Review **Changes** for observed before and after values. A missing row
   in a snapshot comparison needs corroboration before concluding deletion.
   In **Disabled but active**, check the evidence of activity after disablement
   and look for any observed re-enable.
