# Home

[Русская версия](../ru/home.md) · [Work screens](user-guide.md)

Open **Home** at `/app/`. Use it to choose a domain and see what needs
attention in the selected period.

1. Select an available domain and **Last 24 hours** or **Last 7 days**.
   Select **Previous period** to review the preceding interval of the same
   length. Check the dates shown in the header.
2. Open a problem in **New and important in this period** to review its
   evidence in [Problems](problems.md). For activity, compare the series
   for successful and failed logons in the selected period.
3. **Domain controller status** links to [AD state](ad-state.md).
   **Permission and configuration changes** links to
   [Domain changes](domain-changes.md). Follow the available row or panel
   links to examine the records behind the summary.
4. Use **Refresh** to reload. If a panel has missing data or a load error,
   check that panel and the domain's observation health before interpreting
   a quiet period.
